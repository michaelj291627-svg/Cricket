General Rules
Each team must have a minimum of 6 registered players.
All players must be women.
No player can represent more than one team during the tournament.
Teams should report to the venue at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.
Umpire decisions will be considered final and binding.
Fair play and sportsmanship are expected from all participants.
Players participate at their own risk. The organizers will not be responsible for injuries or loss of personal belongings.
